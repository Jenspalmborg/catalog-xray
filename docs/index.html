See what your store is missing.

Paste a store’s address or upload its product export. Catalog X-ray compares the range with what shoppers actually buy and tells you what to add, which keywords each product lacks, and which searches disappoint.

What the report tells you

Each finding comes from BehaviorGPT, a model trained on long sequences of what shoppers view, add to cart and buy.

What to add

Kinds of products shoppers look at alongside or right after yours, that you don’t carry, with the styles they pick and a market price range.

Jeans · slim fit, ripped, skinny, stretch · $23–44

Keywords to add

Words shoppers use for products like yours that don’t find your product in your own search today. Ready to paste into tags.

8-person camping tent: + cabin tent + instant

Assortment balance

Your share of the range per product area, next to that area’s share of what shoppers buy in your part of the market.

Shoes: 23% of the range, 6% of demand

Search QA

Your key searches, run against your own catalog. Weak ones return the wrong kind of product, or nothing that matches.

“performance”: 1 of top 5 results mention it

Gentle by design

To avoid loading a store or getting blocked, Catalog X-ray reads products the lightest way available and only falls back when it has to. Every request follows robots.txt and is clearly identified.

Run it on your store

It runs on your own machine with your own BehaviorGPT key (get one at unboxai.com/behaviorgpt). Needs Python 3.11+ and uv.

# get it
git clone https://github.com/Jenspalmborg/catalog-xray.git
cd catalog-xray
uv sync
cp .env.example .env            # paste your key as UNBOXAI_API_KEY

# open the app in your browser: paste a URL or upload an export
uv run xray-web

# or from the command line
uv run xray https://www.some-store.com
uv run xray --file products.csv --name "My store"

A first scan takes a few minutes, mostly for uploading the catalog to BehaviorGPT. Reports are a single HTML page plus CSV exports. Your key holds one catalog at a time, so each new store replaces the previous upload.
